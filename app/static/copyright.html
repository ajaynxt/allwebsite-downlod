Responsible use

Copyright & takedown

AJAYNXT does not grant users rights to third-party media. The downloader is intended for owned or permitted public content.

Report a concern

Email __CONTACT_EMAIL__ with: your name and contact details; the copyrighted work; the exact URL involved; a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized; and a statement that your information is accurate.

What happens next

A complete notice can be reviewed, relevant access can be limited where technically applicable, and repeat abuse can be blocked. Temporary download jobs normally expire automatically, so include enough information to identify the issue.

Do not submit false notices

Only the rights holder or an authorized representative should send a copyright notice. Seek independent legal advice if ownership or permitted use is unclear.
