Useful answers

Before you paste.

Does every website work?+

Thousands of public sites and generic embedded media can work, but private/login-only, DRM-protected, expired or platform-blocked links may not.

Why can a Reel fail?+

Instagram and similar platforms frequently change access rules. A public single-Reel link works best; login-only content is intentionally not accessed.

Can I save a Pinterest video Pin?+

A public single-Pin URL can work when the Pin exposes downloadable media. Private boards, login-only Pins and whole-board downloads are not supported.

Is “Best Quality” always 4K?+

No. It selects the highest quality actually offered for that media. If the source is 1080p, the output cannot truthfully become native 4K.

What am I allowed to download?+

Only content you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally entitled to download. Platform terms and local law still apply.
