Privacy

A TO Z Link Downloader is owned by AJAYNXT. The app is designed to process links temporarily, without building a permanent media library.

What is processed

When you submit a link, the server processes that URL, basic request information needed for abuse prevention, and temporary media files required to complete your request.

Retention

Prepared files and in-memory job details expire automatically after the configured job period, normally one hour. Server operators may retain limited security logs for reliability and abuse investigation.

Accounts, analytics and ads

The core downloader does not require an account. Analytics or advertising must be enabled separately by the site owner and should be disclosed through an appropriate consent notice before non-essential tracking is used.

Third-party sources

The link you submit is requested from its source platform. That platform processes the request under its own privacy terms.
