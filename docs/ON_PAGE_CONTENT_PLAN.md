# On-page content plan

## Home

- Lead with the working action: paste a permitted public link.
- State quality truthfully: best available from the source, never artificial 4K.
- Show major platform categories and link to one authoritative support page.
- Keep responsible-use confirmation beside the download action.
- Show owner, direct UPI support, editable AJAYNXT ad and legal trust links.

## Future editorial content

Publish only tested, original help content: how public links behave, why a source fails, source-quality explanations, and safe troubleshooting. Do not mass-produce city/platform/format doorway pages or promise universal support.
