Supported public links

Many platforms.
Clear limits.

A TO Z Link Downloader reads single public media links. Support is source-dependent and can change when platforms update access rules.

YouTube

Public single-video links, available video qualities and audio output. Playlists, private videos and live streams are excluded.

Instagram

Public single Reels or posts when media is accessible without a login. Private accounts are never accessed.

Pinterest

Public single video Pins when the Pin exposes media. Private boards and full-board downloads are excluded.

Facebook

Public single video or Reel links. Login-only, restricted and private posts may fail.

TikTok

Public single-video URLs, subject to region and platform access rules.

X / Twitter

Public posts containing accessible video. Protected accounts are not supported.

More public sources

Reddit, Vimeo, SoundCloud, LinkedIn, Snapchat Spotlight and many extractor-supported websites.

Direct media

Public direct video or audio URLs and compatible pages with exposed media streams.

Intentionally excluded

Private, DRM or bulk content.

  • Private or login-only media
  • DRM-protected sources
  • Playlists, channels, profiles or boards
  • Live or expired media
  • Content without your permission
